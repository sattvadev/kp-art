Leave tracing?

You'll go back to the start screen. Your photo and drawing are saved on this device: Continue where you left off opens them again.

How to trace

  • Set up: prop the phone 25–40 cm above the paper, camera facing down.
  • On iPad: tap Draw on screen with Apple Pencil on the start screen. The Pencil draws with the brushes, eraser, fill, colour, settings and layers on the left; two fingers move and zoom; tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo. On a phone, your finger draws. Select on the rail: draw a loop, then drag, scale or turn it. Layers: add, lock, blend, merge and reorder. Touch and hold to pick a colour.
  • Place the picture: drag, pinch and twist it onto the paper. Tap Move first if it is locked.
  • Lock it: tap Lock. Now a pinch zooms in on details and the picture stays lined up. Double-tap to zoom out.
  • Steps: in Values or AI outlines, tap a number at the bottom to jump to that step, lightest first. All shows everything.
  • Tools has the view (photo, outlines, values), grid, strobe, camera and flashlight. Tap Done or the picture to close it.
  • Grid: in Tools → Grid, put the grid on the photo. It sticks to the picture as you move, scale or turn it. Draw the same squares on your paper to keep proportions and angles right; Diagonals help with perspective.
  • Hide and Opacity help you check your drawing against the paper. Hide leaves the grid showing.
  • Saved as you go: your photo and drawing stay on this device. Home starts a new canvas; the start screen reopens the last one (and the one before).

Photos stay on your phone. AI sketch uses the Informative Drawings model by Caroline Chan, Frédo Durand and Phillip Isola (MIT License), run on your phone.

Camera tracing

KP Art

Your picture floats over the live camera, so you can trace it onto paper while watching your pencil on screen.

Last session
  1. Prop your phone 25–40 cm above the paper, camera facing down (a stand, a glass or a stack of books).
  2. Choose a photo, then start the camera.
  3. Drag and pinch the picture to fit the paper, then tap Lock. Tap Move to place it again.
  4. Pinch to zoom in on details: the picture stays lined up with your paper.
  5. Tools has outlines, values, grid, strobe, camera and flashlight. Tap a number at the bottom to jump between value steps.
  6. On iPad, Draw on screen makes the screen the paper: draw over the picture with Apple Pencil, then save PNG or SVG.
