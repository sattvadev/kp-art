Trace Camera

Your picture floats over the live camera, so you can trace it onto paper while watching your pencil on screen.

  1. Prop your phone 25–40 cm above the paper, camera facing down (a stand, a glass or a stack of books).
  2. Choose a photo, then start the camera.
  3. Drag and pinch the picture to fit the paper, then tap Lock.
  4. Draw while looking at the screen. Hold peek to check your lines.

Photos stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
